## Supporting the complete mission cycle

An autonomous drone needs infrastructure between flights as well as hardware in the air. At avemoy FlexCo, I designed ground support equipment around precision landing, battery charging, and automated data offload for greenhouse operations.

## My contribution

I integrated the UAV's landing, power, and data-transfer requirements into the ground station design. The work connected aircraft development with the equipment needed to support scheduled missions and process collected data.

## Inside the station

The interior photograph shows the power supplies, computing hardware, wiring, and service layout. Packaging these subsystems together makes cable routing, access, and maintenance part of the mechanical design. This page documents the integration work; it does not assign a docking success rate or charging-time specification.
