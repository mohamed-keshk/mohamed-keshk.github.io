## A platform for warehouse robotics

This graduation project explored a mobile industrial robot intended to operate as part of a collaborating fleet in a warehouse. The project combined a mecanum-wheel base, a lifting mechanism, LiDAR, and ROS-based SLAM navigation.

## My contribution within the team

Working in a team of six, I was responsible for designing and manufacturing the complete robot hardware. I iterated the structure and packaging against the team's requirements, integrating the drive system, lifting arrangement, sensors, and onboard electronics.

## From CAD to the assembled platform

The renders expose the chassis and wheel arrangement, while the photographs show the enclosed robot. The project documentation describes a 200 kg payload capacity; no load-test record is presented on this page. Collaborative fleet operation was the intended application.

The Nile University graduation project was funded by ASRT, mentored by Valeo, and received an A+ grade.
