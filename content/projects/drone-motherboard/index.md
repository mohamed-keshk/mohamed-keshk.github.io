## Integrating the drone electronics

This motherboard project explores a common PCB for the electronics and connections of a robotic aircraft. The supplied KiCad views show a component-dense board with a central computing-module area and connectors distributed around its edges.

## Board design views

The top and underside views document component placement, mounting, and the connector layout. They provide a closer look at the electronics design work behind a complete mechatronic system.

**Project stage:** PCB development prototype. These are digital board views; they do not establish an assembled or electrically validated board. Manufacturing readiness and flight qualification require separate checks.
