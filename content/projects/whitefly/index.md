## Aircraft and field packaging

WhiteFly adds another example of compact UAV hardware to my portfolio. The supplied photographs show a built aircraft with a central enclosure, four protected propellers, and a transport case for the equipment.

## Hardware gallery

The images focus on the physical aircraft and how it is carried between work locations. This visual record complements the more detailed [Firefly case study](/projects/firefly/). Performance figures and a development timeline are not documented here.
